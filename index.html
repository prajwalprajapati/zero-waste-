Zero Waste Tips 💡

Small changes can make a big environmental difference.

🛍️

Carry Reusable Bags

Avoid single-use plastic bags by carrying reusable cloth bags.

💧

Use Reusable Bottles

Replace disposable plastic bottles with a reusable water bottle.

🍱

Avoid Food Waste

Plan meals carefully and store leftover food properly.

♻️

Recycle Correctly

Separate recyclable materials from general waste.

🔧

Repair Instead of Replace

Repair clothes, electronics and other products before buying new ones.

🌱

Compost Organic Waste

Turn food scraps and organic waste into useful compost.
