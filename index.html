📚 Waste Sorting Guide

Know where your waste belongs.

🍎

Organic

Fruit and vegetable scraps, food leftovers, leaves and other biodegradable materials.

🥤

Plastic

Bottles, containers and packaging. Clean recyclable plastic before disposal.

📦

Paper

Cardboard, newspapers, notebooks and clean paper products.

💻

E-Waste

Phones, computers, chargers, batteries and electronic devices.

🍾

Glass

Glass bottles and jars. Handle broken glass carefully.

🔩

Metal

Aluminium cans, steel containers and recyclable metal objects.

🗑️

General Waste

Materials that cannot currently be recycled or composted.

⚠️

Hazardous

Batteries, chemicals and other hazardous materials need special disposal.

💡 Eco Tips

Simple habits that make a real difference.

🛍️

Carry Reusable Bags

Keep a cloth bag with you so you don't need single-use plastic bags.

💧

Refill Your Bottle

Use a reusable bottle instead of buying disposable plastic bottles.

🍱

Prevent Food Waste

Plan meals, store food properly and use leftovers creatively.

🔧

Repair Before Replacing

Repair clothes, electronics and furniture whenever practical.

♻️

Separate Waste

Keep organic, recyclable and general waste separated for better processing.

🌱

Compost Organics

Convert suitable food scraps and garden waste into useful compost.
